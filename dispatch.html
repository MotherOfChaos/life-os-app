🔥Heat Dome — Western Europe
Right Outside Your Door
Strongest Heat Dome of 2026 Hits Europe — 12–16°C Above Normal. Barcelona Included.

A powerful subtropical ridge from North Africa has trapped hot air across Western and Central Europe in what forecasters are calling the strongest heat dome recorded so far in 2026. Temperatures are running 12 to 16 degrees Celsius above seasonal norms — meaning what feels like a July heatwave is happening in late May.

The numbers: France broke its all-time May temperature record. The UK hit 32.3°C — its hottest May day in nearly 80 years — and experienced a "tropical night" where temperatures never dropped below 20°C. Portugal hit 40°C. Spain is expected to reach 38°C, with Iberia forecast to face the most prolonged stretch of any region, with temperatures 5–10°C above average through the end of May and into June 1.

Fires broke out in Edinburgh's Holyrood Park. France placed large parts of the country under heat warnings. Météo-France described temperatures in Brittany as "quite remarkable so early in the pre-summer season." Scientists: what was once a July phenomenon is now arriving in mid-May.

Barcelona right now: Late-May heat is running like late July. Expect 30–35°C+, very low humidity, strong sun. Drink water aggressively. Avoid sun exposure 11:00–17:00. Check on elderly neighbours. The terrace garden needs extra monitoring (see below).
Climate context: Heat domes locking in extreme weather have nearly tripled since the 1950s due to human-driven climate change. The three hottest years globally on record were 2024, 2023, and 2025 — in that order. 2026 is on track.

Stalling
Deal "Largely Negotiated" but Three Key Sticking Points Block Signature

After Trump said Saturday the deal was "largely negotiated," he reversed course Sunday saying the US "won't rush." Iran's foreign ministry said they have "reached understandings on a large portion of issues — but no one can claim a signature is imminent." Three sticking points remain unresolved:

1. Hormuz management: Trump says Iran will reopen it; Iran says the Strait will "remain under Iranian management" and the US "has nothing to do with it." Iran insists any mechanism must be agreed with Oman and bordering states, not the US unilaterally.

2. Nuclear programme: Trump wants Iran to renounce nuclear ambitions before signing. Iran wants the war formally ended before nuclear talks begin. Neither side is moving.

3. Sanctions and frozen assets: Iran wants immediate unfreezing of assets and permanent sanctions relief. The US is offering staged "relief for performance." Iran wants the money first.

Meanwhile US and Iranian forces exchanged strikes in late May even as Trump said talks were "proceeding nicely." The Hormuz blockade continues — 10 ships/day vs 130 before. Global inflation remains elevated.

Israel wildcard: Israel remains strongly opposed to any deal that includes halting the Lebanon war. It has continued airstrikes on southern Lebanon despite the US-brokered extension. This is the factor neither Trump nor Iran controls.

🧠Science & Neuroscience
⭐⭐ Major — Aging
Menin Protein Decline Drives Aging — Simple Supplement May Reverse Brain Decline

Researchers discovered a previously unknown protein, Menin, in the hypothalamus — the brain's master regulator of metabolism, sleep, temperature and hunger. When Menin levels decline with age, they trigger a cascade: hypothalamic inflammation, memory problems, bone density loss, and accelerated systemic aging. The finding reframes a large part of why we age the way we do.

The exciting part: in animal models, restoring Menin levels reversed many of these aging markers. Researchers are exploring whether existing supplements targeting this pathway could be effective in humans. The hypothalamus as a "master aging clock" is a significant conceptual shift. (ScienceDaily, May 24)

⭐ Pain Science
Scientists "Recharge" Damaged Nerves to Stop Chronic Pain at Source — Duke University

For the millions experiencing chronic nerve pain — where even soft touch feels agonising — Duke University researchers found that damaged nerves can be revived by essentially "recharging" them. The technique restores normal signalling in sensitised neurons, stopping the pain signal at its source rather than masking it downstream. The approach is radically different from existing pain management and could open a new class of treatments. (ScienceDaily, May 24)

Sleep Sweet Spot: Under 6 or Over 8 Hours Accelerates Organ Aging

A new study found a "U-shaped" relationship between sleep duration and the biological aging of 17 different organ systems. Both sleeping fewer than 6 hours AND more than 8 hours accelerates the aging of the heart, lungs, and immune system. The optimal window is 7–8 hours. The effect is measurable across organ systems — not just brain health — suggesting sleep is a systemic anti-aging mechanism.

Ozempic/Semaglutide: Brain Cells Respond Differently — Explains Why It Works for Some, Not Others

NIH research (May 25) reveals that semaglutide — the active ingredient in Ozempic and Wegovy — triggers fundamentally different responses inside individual appetite-controlling brain cells. This explains the enormous variation in effectiveness between patients, and gives researchers a specific target for developing next-generation versions that work more consistently. It also found a possible mechanism to extend the drug's effectiveness over time before the common plateau effect kicks in.

🌱Terrace Alert — Heat Dome Protocol
Can Booty · Urgent
Heat Dome = Emergency Terrace Check

With temperatures 12–16°C above normal, your terrace garden is under serious stress right now. Key rules during a heat dome:

Water: Double frequency. Morning only (before 09:00) — watering during midday heat scalds roots. Self-watering containers will drain faster than usual.

Seedlings and recent transplants (tomatoes, basil, herbs) are most vulnerable — they haven't established deep roots yet. Check soil moisture twice daily.

Mulch: If you have any straw, bark, or even newspaper, cover the soil surface around pots — reduces evaporation dramatically.

Shade: Anything in direct sun from 11:00–17:00 is getting scorched. Consider temporary shade cloth or moving smaller pots if possible.

Arugula and leafy greens will bolt faster in this heat. Check for flower stalks daily and cut immediately.

Basil actually loves heat — but not drought. Keep it consistently moist. Tomatoes in heat: may drop flowers if soil dries out. Consistent watering is critical for fruit set.
