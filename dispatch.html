Italy · Right Now
Suspected Ebola Case in Cagliari — Patient Isolated, Tests Underway in Rome

Italian health authorities activated Ebola protocols in Cagliari, Sardinia this evening after a patient showing symptoms compatible with the virus was transported to the Santissima Trinità Hospital's infectious diseases unit. Police, fire brigade, and medical staff in full protective equipment attended the patient's home. The patient had recently returned from the DRC.

Italy's Ministry of Health confirmed the patient is symptomatic and in isolation. Tests are being carried out overnight by Rome's Spallanzani Institute — the country's reference centre for high-risk infectious diseases. Results expected by tomorrow morning. PM Meloni has proposed putting border management on the agenda of the European Council meeting of June 18–19.

Context: Global Ebola count as of May 30 — 263 confirmed cases, 43 confirmed deaths in DRC and Uganda. Over 1,100 suspected cases. Two earlier suspected cases in Milan (Lombardy, May 26) tested NEGATIVE. The Africa CDC director warned Friday that "the risk of regional spread is real."

For your Italy trip (June 5–7 Garda, June 7+ Ibiza): Risk remains very low. The case is in Sardinia, not the mainland. Protocols are working exactly as designed. Watch for test results tomorrow — negative is the most likely outcome based on the Milan precedent.
⚔️Iran Deal — Still No Signature
Live · Negotiations Extending
Trump Sends Back Changes — Talks Extend Into Another Week

President Trump met with advisers in the White House Situation Room on Friday saying he would make a "final determination" on the Iran deal. The meeting ended after two hours with no decision. Trump then sent the proposed agreement back with changes demanding tougher language on Iran's nuclear commitments and the reopening of Hormuz as non-negotiable. As of Sunday afternoon, still no decision.

What the MOU would include: lifting constraints on Hormuz navigation, a US blockade lifted, and a 60-day negotiation period on Iran's nuclear programme. What's still unresolved: the fate of Iran's highly enriched uranium stockpile, unfreezing of Iranian funds, and Iran's insistence that Hormuz remains under Iranian management — not US control.

Israel continued airstrikes on southern Lebanon on May 30 in what it called an "expansion of operations" — potentially the most significant remaining obstacle to any deal, since Iran has tied Lebanon to negotiations.

Bottom line: Deal is alive but extending into next week. Markets have largely priced in an eventual agreement — oil fell sharply this week on optimism. But "largely negotiated" has been said before, and each delay costs the global economy roughly $4 billion/day in elevated energy costs.

Israel Expands Lebanon Campaign — Ceasefire Increasingly Nominal

Israel warned its public to "expect repeated firing" on May 30 as it announced the expansion of IDF operations in southern Lebanon. The move violates the spirit of the November 2024 ceasefire and has been ongoing through May. Israeli airstrikes hit Kfar Tebnit in southern Lebanon as recently as yesterday. International condemnation has intensified but produced no change in Israeli military behaviour. Hezbollah has not yet responded with full-scale retaliation.

Barcelona Named Among Top Tourism Targets for New EU Entry Fees

May 2026 marked what analysts are calling a "watershed moment" in European overtourism management. Barcelona is implementing Airbnb restrictions and tourist tax hikes simultaneously — addressing both the housing crisis and visitor overload. Venice charges €5 entry for day-trippers. Rome, Athens, Santorini all implementing new visitor levies. The EU is coordinating for the first time on a shared overtourism policy framework, to be discussed at the June 18–19 European Council.

Asian Tiger Mosquito Now Established in Berlin — Europe's Pest Season Expanding

Euronews reports the Asian tiger mosquito (Aedes albopictus), carrier of dengue, chikungunya, and Zika, is now established in Berlin — once considered too cold. The species has been steadily moving north through France, Switzerland, Austria, and now Germany. Barcelona has had established populations since the mid-2010s. The heat dome of May 2026 will accelerate breeding conditions across the continent.
